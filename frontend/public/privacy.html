14. Account deletion and the seven-day deletion hold

You can initiate permanent account deletion through the RomBuzz app. If you no longer have access to the app, see our public Account Deletion & Data Erasure Information page for the external request process.

When an authenticated deletion request is accepted, the account enters an irreversible pending-deletion lifecycle. The user-facing profile is removed from normal RomBuzz experiences and supported user-facing data is deleted, scrubbed, or scheduled for cleanup.

RomBuzz currently maintains the account email in a restricted seven-day deletion hold for deletion processing, account-lifecycle integrity, security, and anti-abuse purposes. This is not a recovery or restoration period. RomBuzz does not provide a flow to restore the deleted account during the hold.

After the hold and required cleanup, the remaining pending-deletion account record is permanently removed. If a technical cleanup step fails, RomBuzz may retain only the limited information needed to retry that cleanup and may postpone the final record deletion until the cleanup succeeds.
