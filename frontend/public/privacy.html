Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RomBuzz collects, uses, stores, protects, shares, and deletes information when you use the RomBuzz dating application, website, and related services.

Effective date: August 22, 2026

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the RomBuzz mobile application, rombuzz.com, and related RomBuzz services.

In this policy, “RomBuzz,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the RomBuzz service and its operator.

2. Age requirement

RomBuzz is an adult dating service intended only for people who are at least 18 years old.

You may not create or use a RomBuzz account if you are under 18. We may suspend or remove accounts when we reasonably believe a user is underage.

3. Information we collect

Account information

We may collect your email address, account identifier, authentication information, account status, and information needed to secure and manage your account.

If you use Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, we receive account information made available by that provider, such as an account identifier, email address, and basic profile information. Apple may provide a private relay email address.

Profile and dating information

Depending on what you choose to provide, RomBuzz may process information such as your name, date of birth, gender, pronouns, orientation, dating preferences, relationship preferences, biography, interests, hobbies, lifestyle information, education, occupation, languages, profile photos, voice introduction, and other profile fields.

Location information

RomBuzz may process location information when you enable location-based features such as nearby discovery, MicroBuzz, distance calculations, or meeting-related features.

Depending on your device permission and feature, this information may include device coordinates, city or general-area information, and derived distance information.

Photos, video, and audio

We process content you upload or create through RomBuzz, including profile photos, gallery media, reels or videos, chat attachments, audio, voice introductions, story content where available, MicroBuzz selfies, and other user-created media.

Messages and interactions

We process information necessary to provide matching and communication features, including matches, likes or Buzz interactions, chat messages, reactions, read status, blocks, reports, gifts, and other interactions with users.

Video calls

RomBuzz may use third-party real-time communication infrastructure, including Agora, to provide one-to-one audio and video call functionality.

Safety and support information

If you report another user, contact support, or become involved in a safety or moderation review, we may collect the report, relevant user and content identifiers, messages you provide to support, moderation decisions, and related records.

Technical information

We may process technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, authentication and security events, network information, and crash or error diagnostics.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure RomBuzz accounts.
  • Provide profile, matching, discovery, chat, media, video-call, notification, and other app features.
  • Personalize relevant dating and discovery experiences.
  • Detect abuse, spam, fraud, harassment, unauthorized activity, and violations of our rules.
  • Process blocks, reports, safety reviews, and support requests.
  • Maintain service reliability, diagnose errors, and improve performance.
  • Comply with legal requirements and protect users, RomBuzz, and others.

5. Service providers

RomBuzz relies on service providers to operate parts of the service. Depending on the feature you use, providers may include:

  • MongoDB Atlas for database infrastructure.
  • Cloudflare R2 for media/object storage.
  • Cloudflare Stream for supported video processing and delivery.
  • Cloudinary for certain existing or legacy media flows and media assets.
  • Agora for real-time audio/video calling infrastructure.
  • Resend for authentication and service-related email.
  • Google and Apple when you choose their authentication services.
  • Render for backend/service hosting.
  • Vercel for website hosting.
  • Sentry, where enabled, for technical crash and error diagnostics.

These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to RomBuzz, subject to their applicable agreements, safeguards, and legal obligations.

6. When information may be shared

Information may be shared with other RomBuzz users when that sharing is part of the feature you use. For example, profile information you make visible, content you post, and messages you send are provided to their intended audience.

We may also disclose information to service providers described above, when required by law, or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, safety threats, illegal activity, or violations of RomBuzz rules.

7. Device permissions

Certain features may ask for access to device capabilities such as your location, camera, microphone, photo library, or notifications.

You can control many of these permissions through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

8. Data retention and account deletion

You can initiate deletion from RomBuzz through:

  • Settings → Delete Account inside the mobile app.
  • Our public account-deletion resource at rombuzz.com/delete-account .

When an authenticated in-app deletion request is accepted, the account is placed into a pending deletion state and is removed from normal RomBuzz user-facing experiences.

RomBuzz currently maintains a seven-day deletion hold. During that period, the account cannot be used normally and the email address and minimal deletion metadata may be retained for deletion, anti-abuse, security, and account-lifecycle purposes.

Associated user-facing records and supported media are deleted or scheduled for cleanup. If an external storage deletion fails temporarily, RomBuzz may retry that deletion before the final pending account record is removed.

Final deletion therefore occurs after the applicable hold period and successful completion of required cleanup operations rather than necessarily at the exact second the seven-day period ends.

Some limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, moderation and safety investigations, support history, financial or accounting records, dispute handling, or legal/regulatory obligations. Where appropriate, retained audit records may be pseudonymized or detached from the active account.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection to certain processing of your personal information.

You can update many profile details directly inside RomBuzz. For other privacy requests, contact: support@rombuzz.com .

10. Security

RomBuzz uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect account and user data, including authenticated access controls, encrypted network transport, restricted service credentials, and security controls around sensitive backend functions.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting access to your device, email account, and RomBuzz credentials.

11. International processing

RomBuzz and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection laws may differ between jurisdictions.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as RomBuzz, applicable law, or our service providers change. When appropriate, we will update the effective date and provide additional notice.
