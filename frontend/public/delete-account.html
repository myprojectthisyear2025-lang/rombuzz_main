Account & Data

Delete your RomBuzz account

You can request permanent deletion of your RomBuzz account and associated personal data even if you no longer have the RomBuzz app installed.

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Delete inside the RomBuzz app

If you still have access to RomBuzz, this is the fastest way to initiate deletion.

Open RomBuzz.
Open Settings.
Find the Danger zone.
Select Delete account.
Review the deletion and BuzzCoin forfeiture information, then confirm deletion.

Don't have the app?

You do not need to reinstall RomBuzz.

Send an account-deletion request to RomBuzz Support from the email address associated with your RomBuzz account.

Request deletion by email

Email: support@rombuzz.com

Use the subject: RomBuzz Account Deletion Request.

For account security, RomBuzz may ask you to verify that you control the account or its registered email address before a support-initiated deletion is processed.

What happens after deletion begins?

Once an authenticated deletion request is accepted, the account is placed into a pending deletion state and removed from normal RomBuzz user-facing experiences.

RomBuzz currently applies a seven-day deletion hold.

During this period, your email address and minimal account-deletion metadata may remain temporarily stored for account lifecycle, security, anti-abuse, and deletion processing purposes.

User-facing profile data, supported media, messages, matches, and related feature records are removed or scheduled for deletion through the account deletion process.

If deletion from an external storage provider temporarily fails, RomBuzz may retry that cleanup before removing the final pending account record.

Data that may be retained

Limited information may be retained when reasonably necessary for:

  • Security and fraud prevention.
  • Safety and moderation investigations.
  • Legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Support and dispute handling.
  • Financial, transaction, or accounting records where applicable.

Where appropriate, retained audit records may be pseudonymized or detached from the deleted active account.

BuzzCoins

Any remaining BuzzCoin or eligible creator balance associated with an account may be forfeited when permanent deletion is confirmed.

The RomBuzz in-app deletion process shows this information before deletion when a balance is present.
