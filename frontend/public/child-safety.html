Child Safety Standards

RomBuzz is an adults-only dating and social platform. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation and to responding appropriately when potentially harmful or illegal activity is reported or identified.

Effective date: September 20, 2026
Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. RomBuzz is for adults only

You must be at least 18 years old to use RomBuzz.

People under 18 are not permitted to create, maintain, or use a RomBuzz account.

Users may not misrepresent their age or assist a person under 18 in accessing RomBuzz. Accounts reasonably believed to belong to minors may be restricted, investigated, suspended, or removed.

2. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

RomBuzz prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, commonly referred to as CSAE, in any form.

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:

  • Child sexual abuse material or CSAM.
  • Sexual exploitation or sexualization of a minor.
  • Grooming a minor for sexual activity or exploitation.
  • Sextortion involving a child.
  • Sex trafficking or attempted trafficking of a child.
  • Requests for sexual images or sexual activity involving minors.
  • Sharing, requesting, producing, promoting, possessing, or distributing CSAM through RomBuzz.
  • Using RomBuzz to arrange, encourage, facilitate, or normalize the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child.

These rules apply to profiles, photos, videos, audio, messages, comments, posts, reels, usernames, biographies, links, and every other form of content or communication available through RomBuzz.

3. Reporting safety concerns

RomBuzz provides in-app reporting tools that users can use to report profiles, users, content, or behavior that may violate our rules.

Reports involving suspected underage users, child sexual exploitation, grooming, CSAM, trafficking, or other threats involving children are treated as serious safety matters.

You may also report a child-safety concern directly to: legal@neptrixx.com.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement or emergency services immediately.

4. How RomBuzz responds

When RomBuzz receives a credible report or otherwise obtains actual knowledge of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we may take actions including:

  • Removing or disabling access to prohibited content.
  • Restricting, suspending, or permanently banning accounts.
  • Preventing repeat access where reasonably possible.
  • Preserving relevant information when required by law.
  • Reviewing related accounts or activity for additional risk.
  • Cooperating with law enforcement and appropriate child-safety authorities where required or legally appropriate.

Serious child-safety violations may result in immediate account removal without prior warning.

5. CSAM and legal reporting

RomBuzz does not permit CSAM on its services.

When RomBuzz confirms or becomes legally required to report apparent CSAM, we will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law, including reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children where applicable or to the appropriate regional authority.

RomBuzz may preserve relevant account or content information when legally required for safety investigations, mandatory reporting, or cooperation with lawful investigations.

6. Underage accounts

If we reasonably determine that a person using RomBuzz is under 18, we may disable or remove the account and take additional measures appropriate to the circumstances.

If you believe someone on RomBuzz is under 18, report the profile through the app or contact our safety team.

8. Related RomBuzz policies

These Child Safety Standards should be read together with our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Violations of these standards may also violate our Terms of Service and may result in immediate enforcement action.

9. Updates to these standards

RomBuzz may update these standards as our services, safety practices, legal obligations, and regulatory requirements evolve.

The current version will always be published at rombuzz.com/child-safety.
